## Not vendored, and deliberately so

The spinor enumeration archive (`self_dual_5_invariant_enumerator`) is
third-party work whose redistribution has not been authorised. It is **not** in
this repository and **not** in any release. What ships instead is a manifest of
per-file hashes, the expected local path, and adapter instructions, so a reader
holding their own copy can reproduce the archive-dependent results and verify
their copy matches the one used here.

`third_party/README.md` records the standing rule: nothing is vendored into that
directory without its author, source revision and licence recorded first.

# The M-only quotient rank

**Result at degree 10: rank 0.** Every degree-10 quotient class requires at
least one N channel. This is conclusion **A** of the three permitted outcomes,
and it bounds the Level-B search before any large enumeration.

Degree 12: pilot pending (see §4).

Reproduce:

    .venv/bin/python scripts/test_M_only_quotients.py --degree 10 \
        --out results/intrinsic_candidates/M_only_quotient_test_deg10.json

## 1. The complete M-only family

Scalars built purely from the symmetric matrix `M` are generated by traces of
its powers. `M` is quadratic in `F`, so five-form degree 2k is M-degree k, and
`tr(M) = 0` removes every partition part of size 1.

| five-form degree | M-degree | complete monomial list |
|---:|---:|---|
| 10 | 5 | `tr(M^5)`, `tr(M^3)tr(M^2)` |
| 12 | 6 | `tr(M^6)`, `tr(M^4)tr(M^2)`, `tr(M^3)^2`, `tr(M^2)^3` |

These are the partitions of 5 and 6 with all parts ≥ 2 — exhaustive within the
M-trace family, not a sample.

## 2. Method

Exact over F_p, no floating point:

1. build the atlas-basis value matrix at `dim A_d + 8` generic samples;
2. evaluate each M-trace monomial at the same samples;
3. solve for atlas coordinates **exactly** — an inconsistent system is
   reported as `not_in_atlas_span`, never approximated by least squares;
4. project into `Q_d` using the echelon form of `D_d`;
5. take the rank of the projections.

## 3. Degree-10 result

| monomial | status | quotient vector | nonzero? |
|---|---|---|---|
| `tr(M^5)` | solved | `[0, 0, 0]` | no |
| `tr(M^3)tr(M^2)` | solved | `[0, 0, 0]` | no |

**M-only quotient rank = 0**, identical at primes 32749, 32719, 32693, 32771,
32713 (fit) and 32717 (holdout).

The result is **not vacuous**. Both monomials *solved* — they lie in the atlas
span — and their quotient vectors are exactly zero. So both lie entirely inside
the reachable closure `D_10`. Had they merely failed to solve, the rank would
also have read 0, for an entirely different and uninformative reason; the
`status` field distinguishes the two cases and is recorded per monomial.

## 4. Consequence for the Level-B search

Combined with the earlier exclusion of products (C-QUOT-02: every product basis
entry lies inside `D_d`), two of the candidate families named in the Phase-1
plan are now eliminated at degree 10:

| family | status at degree 10 |
|---|---|
| products of lower intrinsics | **excluded** (C-QUOT-02) |
| M-only trace monomials | **excluded** (this result) |
| N-channel structures (N^(1050), N^(4125), mixed M–N) | **required** |

So any Level-B representative of a degree-10 quotient class must contain at
least one N channel. That is a structural constraint obtained exactly, and it
narrows the block-multiset enumeration `M^a (N1050)^b (N4125)^c` with
`a + b + c = 5` from all multisets to those with `b + c >= 1`.

## 5. A near-miss worth recording

The first implementation read the atlas from
`results/10d_order{d}.json → generators`, which returns 12 of the 14
degree-10 basis elements — the two `lower_product` entries carry no graph. It
reported `product_entries_unresolved` rather than proceeding on a 12-column
solve.

That mattered. A 12-column solve against a 14-dimensional space would have
produced a clean-looking coordinate vector and a plausible quotient rank that
was simply wrong. The registry carries both graph labels and factor lists, so
products now resolve recursively through their factors.

## 6. Scope

- `MOD-CERT`: identical at six primes, which is strong evidence but not a
  characteristic-zero proof (PO-09).
- Generic samples only; non-generic loci are untested (C-SCOPE-02).
- Degree 12 is **not** yet computed. The atlas there has 72 elements, so the
  value matrix needs ~80 samples x 72 evaluations per prime; a bounded pilot
  must measure throughput before committing to all primes.

# Third-party archive boundary

## What the archive is

`self_dual_5_invariant_enumerator` is a spinor-variable enumeration implementation
written by someone else. Its logs contain Windows paths under another person's
home directory. It is not this project's work and its redistribution permission
has not been established.

## The rule

**The archive is never pushed, never released, and never vendored.** Not its
source, not its ZIP, not substantial extracts of its code, not its raw run
outputs.

Enforcement is mechanical, not a promise:

- `scripts/build_release_candidate.py` copies an explicit allowlist and fails the
  build on any secret pattern or absolute home path.
- `scripts/verify_archive.py` lets a reader check their own copy against a
  manifest of hashes without the archive ever being distributed.
- The archive work copy lives **outside** the repository tree entirely, so it
  cannot be added by a careless `git add -A`.

## What IS published, and why it is permissible

| published | why |
|---|---|
| a manifest of file paths, sizes and SHA-256 hashes | hashes of a file are not the file; they let a reader verify their own copy |
| our own audit summary describing the archive's structure | our words about someone else's code, not their code |
| our own independent re-implementation of the mathematics | the mathematics is not copyrightable; the expression is, and none is copied |
| numerical results computed *from* the archive | outputs of a computation, not the program |

## The distinction that matters

The bridge package re-implements the *mathematics* the archive describes --- the
oscillator realisation, the port-graph contraction rule, the tensor-word
construction --- in exact modular arithmetic, from the mathematical description.
It does not copy implementation text. `docs/SOURCE_OVERLAP_AUDIT.md` records the
difference between allowed mathematical overlap and copied expression, checked
mechanically.

## Reproducibility consequence, stated plainly

Results that depend on the archive's *selected candidate list* cannot be
regenerated by a reader who does not hold the archive. Those are named
explicitly in the data availability statement. Every other result --- the atlas,
the incidence table, the product decomposition, the bridge, the common-sample
comparison --- reproduces without it.

## Unresolved

Redistribution permission has not been sought or granted. Until it is, this
boundary is permanent. Recorded as review item G-7.

# The intrinsic sextic quotient and its stress-flow role

## Result proved at this stage

Let \(\mathcal A_6\) be the two-dimensional vector space of homogeneous
Lorentz-scalar polynomials of degree six in a self-dual five-form
\(\Lambda\).  Define the two tensorial scalars

\[
J_6:=\operatorname{Tr}(M^3)
=M_\mu{}^\nu M_\nu{}^\rho M_\rho{}^\mu
\]

and

\[
K_6:=
N^{(1050)}_{[\rho_1\rho_2\rho_3,\rho_4\rho_5]\kappa}
N_{(1050)}^{[\rho_1\rho_2\rho_3,}{}_
 {\alpha_1\alpha_2]\alpha_3}
N_{(1050)}^{[\rho_4\rho_5\kappa,\alpha_1\alpha_3]\alpha_2},
\]

where normalized antisymmetrization is used and

\[
N^{(1050)}_{[abc,de]f}
=\Lambda^{mn}{}_{[abc}\Lambda_{de]fmn}.
\]

These are equations (2.13)--(2.15) of
[Hutomo--Lechner--Sorokin](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.14351v2) and the
sixth-order basis in
[Cederwall et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.14350v2).

The exact result is:

> **Intrinsic sextic-basis theorem.**
> \((J_6,K_6)\) is a basis of \(\mathcal A_6\).  The free-stress sextic
> subspace is \(\langle J_6\rangle\).  Therefore
> \(\mathcal A_6/\langle J_6\rangle\) is one-dimensional and is generated by
> the nonzero intrinsic class \([K_6]\).

This theorem identifies the quotient direction without using the arbitrary
repository name `I6_2`.  It is a theorem about the static free-stress algebra
and its tangent space.  It is not, by itself, a universal dynamical
obstruction theorem.

## Exact relation to the verified graph basis

In the ordered repository basis \((I6_1,I6_2)\),

\[
\begin{pmatrix}J_6&K_6\end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix}I6_1&I6_2\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}
\frac{32}{3}&-\frac1{1125}\\[2mm]
0&\frac3{125}
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Equivalently, for a polynomial

\[
V_6=c_1 I6_1+c_2 I6_2=aJ_6+bK_6,
\]

\[
\begin{pmatrix}a\\b\end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix}
\frac3{32}&\frac1{288}\\[2mm]
0&\frac{125}{3}
\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}c_1\\c_2\end{pmatrix}.
\]

The determinant of the first matrix is \(32/125\ne0\).  This gives both an
analytic independence proof and an exact inverse.

The repository label `I6_1` is therefore not literally the paper-normalized
\(\operatorname{Tr}(M^3)\); it is \(3J_6/32\).  Likewise, `I6_2` is not
literally \(K_6\); it differs by normalization and a \(J_6\) admixture.

## Quotient coordinate and projector

Normalize the quotient map by \(q_6([K_6])=1\):

\[
q_6:\mathcal A_6\longrightarrow\mathbb Q,\qquad
q_6(aJ_6+bK_6)=b.
\]

In repository coordinates this is the exact covector

\[
q_6(c_1,c_2)=\frac{125}{3}c_2.
\]

An intrinsic direct-sum projector associated with the tensorial complement
\(\langle K_6\rangle\) is

\[
P_{K}(V_6)=q_6(V_6)K_6,\qquad
\ker P_K=\langle J_6\rangle.
\]

Under an arbitrary invertible change of sextic basis, the coordinate column
and the row covector \(q_6\) transform contragrediently, so the scalar
\(q_6(V_6)\), its vanishing, and the quotient class are unchanged.  Under a
nonzero field rescaling \(\Lambda\mapsto s\Lambda\), both \(J_6\) and \(K_6\)
scale by \(s^6\); membership in \(\langle J_6\rangle\) and nonvanishing of the
quotient class are again preserved.

This establishes basis and field-normalization independence.  Invariance
under nonlinear field redefinitions, equations-of-motion quotients, and the
INZ/PST/clone formulation dictionary is a separate dynamical question and is
not inferred here.

## Spinor form

The self-dual five-form is equivalently the constrained symmetric chiral
bispinor

\[
F^{ab}=\frac1{5!}F_{\mu(5)}
(\widetilde{\sigma}^{\mu(5)})^{ab},
\qquad F^{ab}(\sigma_\mu)_{ab}=0.
\]

Section “\(F_5\) invariants in spinor formalism” of
[arXiv:2509.14350v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.14350v2) gives a spinor
sextic basis \((\Sigma_1,\Sigma_2)\), with

\[
\Sigma_1=\operatorname{Tr}(H^3)
\]

and

\[
\Sigma_2=
\Omega^{a_1a_2,a_3a_4}
I_{b_1a_1,a_3b_2}I_{c_1a_2,a_4c_2}
F^{b_1c_1}F^{b_2c_2}.
\]

The same source expresses \(N^{(1050)}\) linearly in the spinor bilinear
\(H\), so substituting that expression into the definition of \(K_6\) is an
explicit spinor formula for the quotient representative.  It does not
publish the numerical change of basis between
\((\Sigma_1,\Sigma_2)\) and \((J_6,K_6)\); no coefficient for that uncomputed
map is claimed here.

## Precisely scoped obstruction corollary

For the free auxiliary-field seed,

\[
T_{\mu\nu}^{(0)}=\frac1{48}M_{\mu\nu},\qquad
\left.\operatorname{Tr}(T^3)\right|_{d=6}
=\frac{J_6}{48^3}.
\]

Every polynomial scalar made from the free stress tensor at field degree six
is therefore proportional to \(J_6\), so its quotient coordinate vanishes:

\[
q_6\!\left(
\left.f(T^{(0)})\right|_{d=6}
\right)=0.
\]

Hence a first-order deformation tangent at the free seed cannot have a
nonzero \([K_6]\) component if its generator is analytic in polynomial
stress invariants.  Whether interacting traces later source this class, or
whether it propagates from a nonzero seed value, requires the full
coefficient equations and is intentionally left open at this stage.

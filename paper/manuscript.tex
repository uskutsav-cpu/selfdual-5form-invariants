\begin{abstract}
We give a reproducible certificate for 81 explicit contractions of a real
self-dual five-form in signature $(1,9)$. The fixed selection consists of one
quartic, two sextic, six octic, twelve degree-ten, and sixty degree-twelve
polynomials. Their Jacobian has a nonzero $81\times81$ minor at four exact
finite-field specializations, using the same ordered functions in every
case. An integral coordinate convention makes each nonzero minor a
characteristic-zero independence witness when its entries are verified as
derivatives of the specified contractions. An independent implementation
checks all Jacobian entries at two further points, and an integral orbit-action
certificate supplies the matching upper bound. This gives a maximal generically independent
family. It does not determine all polynomial generators or syzygies.
\end{abstract}

\section{Scope and conventions}
Let $F$ be an alternating five-form, with metric
$\eta=\operatorname{diag}(-1,1,\ldots,1)$ and Hodge convention
$\star F=F$. The self-dual subspace has dimension 126.
The literature supplies the generic quotient dimension 81 and the
low-degree Hilbert coefficients \cite{cederwall,hutomo}. The present
certificates check the orbit dimension and matching lower bound using explicit graph
contractions. The contraction catalogs and previously discovered bases are
inherited from the repository's research branches; the certificate reported
here re-evaluates those functions at new points.
Graph enumeration and tensor-network contraction followed by numerical linear
algebra are established methods for tensor invariants: see
Elamaran, Ferko and Scarlett \cite{elamaran}, whose six-dimensional three-form
study leaves the ten-dimensional chiral five-form application for future work.
We make no claim of priority for this method or for an 81-invariant family.

A graph has one vertex per copy of $F$, no loops, and valence five.
An edge of multiplicity $m$ contracts $m$ index pairs with inverse metrics.
Vertices and edges are ordered; slot order is fixed by lexicographic edge
order. Graph isomorphism alone does not fix the sign of an odd-form
contraction, so the formula identifier hashes the ordered edge record.
Disconnected contractions are products. The quadratic contraction vanishes
on this self-dual space: $F\wedge\star F=F\wedge F=0$, since $F$ has odd
degree, so its metric norm vanishes. Complete contraction with the invariant
metric proves Lorentz invariance of each graph polynomial.

For each increasing five-tuple $I$ containing 0, define
$A_I=F_I$ and let $e_I$ denote its compact alternating component vector.
The integral parametrization is
\[
 F(A)=\sum_{I\ni0} A_I(e_I+\star e_I).
\]
The 126 columns contain only $0,1,-1$; their components indexed by $I\ni0$
form an identity matrix. Every graph polynomial therefore has integer
coefficients in $A$, with no projector denominators.

The determinant is recomputed by Python-integer elimination independently
of the streaming rank sieve. Euler's identity
$\sum_I A_I J_{aI}=\deg(G_a)G_a$ is checked for every row.

Suppose the recorded entries have been verified as the Jacobian of the
specified integral graph polynomials at the recorded point. A nonzero minor
modulo $p$ then proves that the corresponding integer determinant polynomial
is not identically zero. In characteristic zero the Jacobian criterion gives
algebraic independence of these 81 polynomials, and their differentials are
independent on the nonempty Zariski-open locus where that minor is nonzero.
Invariance and the generic orbit dimension supply the matching upper bound.
The independent audit companion
\path{results/audit/root-review/orbit_tangent_certificate.json} records the 45 infinitesimal
Lorentz generators acting on the 126 integral self-dual coordinates, using a
separate signed-component implementation. Its $45\times45$ minors are nonzero
at primes 50021 and 32749, with residues 5222 and 10852, respectively. The
orbit-action entries are integral linear polynomials in $A$, so the same
nonzero-minor lifting argument proves generic orbit dimension at least 45 in
characteristic zero. There are only 45 generators, hence the generic dimension
is exactly 45. Every invariant differential annihilates this tangent space,
giving the upper bound $126-45=81$. The companion additionally checks that all
81 computed Jacobian rows annihilate all 45 action rows at both points. This
supplies a direct witness for the generic dimension also cited in the literature.
At two additional points generated after the freeze, the same ordered graph
family has nonzero $81\times81$ minor residues 35642 modulo 50021 and 22345
modulo 32749. An independently authored forward-dual and reverse-adjoint
implementation checks all 162 values and all $162\cdot126=20{,}412$ Jacobian
entries. Integer Bareiss elimination independently verifies both minors.
These implementations share NumPy arithmetic primitives but import no
production contraction, Hodge, projection, rank-sieve or verifier code.
On a regular local quotient the selected invariants give local coordinates.
Neither global orbit separation, generation of the invariant field by rational
functions in this list, nor polynomial generation of the entire ring follows.

\section{Polynomial spaces and literature comparison}
The homogeneous-space dimensions reported in equation (4.2) of
\cite{cederwall} through degree twelve are $1,2,7,14,72$.
These representation-theoretic counts are an external input here. Products must be included in value-space comparisons.
At degree twelve the ten product monomials are $I_4^3$, the three symmetric
products of sextics, and the six products $I_4 I_{8,j}$. The independent audit
recomputes this list and the 62 connected graph directions. Concatenating
their gradients at two fresh points within each field gives a $72\times252$
matrix, with nonzero $72\times72$ minor residues 43334 modulo 50021 and 12699
modulo 32749. The integer-minor lifting argument proves linear independence
of the 72 homogeneous polynomials in characteristic zero. The matching
published Hilbert coefficient supplies completeness. The ten independent
products therefore leave 62 primitive quotient directions. This does not
assert algebraic independence of the degree-twelve list. Polynomial-space
evidence uses gradients concatenated at several points; the functional
Jacobian uses one point. These are separate sieves. The prose of section 4.1.4 of
\cite{cederwall} says 64 new degree-twelve directions, inconsistent with its
equation (4.2), which has exponent 62. The inventory here uses the latter;
we do not treat the inconsistent prose as an additional verified count.
Factor exponents in a Hilbert product are not automatically generator counts
at higher degrees when generators and relations may coexist.

The octic comparison uses a selection of six displayed literature
contractions: the first five expressions from equations (4.12)--(4.15) of
\cite{cederwall}, together with the hatted expression (4.18). The
machine-readable matrix has exact rational entries and includes
the quartic-square product explicitly. The fresh source certificate described
below recovers the same matrix as the archived octic map.
The $6\times6$ block compares primitive classes modulo products; any product
correction is retained in the full $7\times7$ matrix.

A source audit found that the antisymmetrizers in the archived implementations
of candidates $J_{10}$--$J_{12}$ differ from the primary PDF and TeX source.
The corrected implementation applies a red antisymmetrization of the trailing
pair in the first factor of $J_{10}$, and of the trailing triple in each of
the final three factors of $J_{11}$ and $J_{12}$. These act after the black
stage and before index raising. The archived readings and atlas remain
separately identified; the source audit failure is preserved.

An independent source evaluator computes the corrected twelve decics and
all twelve displayed octics. The exact certificate uses 14 common binary
self-dual coordinate points under seven primes. Proven denominator and
absolute-value bounds make signed Chinese remaindering uniquely recover
the integer numerator of each rational evaluation; the combined modulus
exceeds twice every bound. Exact graph evaluation matrices have ranks 7 and
14 in degrees eight and ten. Since these equal the published homogeneous
space dimensions, evaluation is injective on those invariant spaces.
Exact rational solving thus determines polynomial coordinates, conditional
on those published upper bounds and analytic tensor invariance. A second
integer/Fraction verifier checks every residue, bound, minor and map; 83
dense finite-field points provide further implementation checks. This
argument uses injectivity and integer bounds, not finite holdouts alone.
The complete evidence is in
\path{results/audit/source-review/exact-source-evaluation-certificate.json}.

The corrected decic $12\times14$ map has rank twelve. Its union with the two
product directions has rank thirteen, so the intersection dimension is one
and its primitive quotient image has dimension eleven. In the recorded
graph normalization, an exact witness is
\[
 J_6+\frac56J_5-\frac1{20}J_2-\frac{11}{1008}J_1
 =-\frac{13}{63}I_{4,1}I_{6,1}.
\]
Consequently these twelve source expressions do not supply an invertible
$12\times12$ map to the twelve graph primitive classes. This corrected
conclusion has fresh evidence; it does not validate the archived J10--J12
transcription. The graph-only nonzero-minor proof has separate inputs.

\section{Reproduction and validation}
Use the declared dependencies and run the regression suite before evaluating
certificates. The original low-degree commit is preserved by
\texttt{v0.1-low-degree-complete}. The release commands are:
\begin{verbatim}
python scripts/graph_to_latex.py
python scripts/search_rank81.py
python scripts/verify_rank81.py
python scripts/verify_rank81.py --recompute
python scripts/verify_independent_audit.py
python scripts/validate_rank81_lorentz.py
python scripts/map_literature_basis.py --degree 8
python scripts/map_literature_basis.py --degree 10
\end{verbatim}
The default verifier checks the saved matrix and metadata; the explicit
\texttt{--recompute} mode repeats the graph evaluations with the production
evaluator. The separate independent audit checker verifies saved integer
minors and source CRT certificates without recomputing tensor contractions.
The source-map commands verify the corrected certificate by default;
historical fitting requires an explicit legacy option and a separate output.
These modes are reported separately. The search stage checkpoints each raw row and its
pivot, checks run identity on resume, and bounds worker submissions.
Degree-specific discovery remains available through the existing nauty
pipelines. \texttt{run\_degree.py} accepts additional edge-list JSONL streams
and separates polynomial completeness from functional rank.

Regression tests cover the 6D reproduction, self-duality,
the zero quadratic contraction, exact integer arithmetic, reverse
Jacobians against an independent oracle, graph parsing beyond ten vertices,
and genuine Lorentz boosts. The independent audit additionally checks every
selected graph under four new spatial rotations and four genuine boosts,
across two primes and fresh points. These checks use exact reduced arithmetic
without floating-point tolerances. They are implementation checks at specified inputs,
while invariance itself follows from the complete-contraction definition.

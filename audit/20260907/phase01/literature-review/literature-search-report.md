## Outcome and source inconsistency

No prior explicit list of 81 functionally independent polynomial invariants of one real self-dual five-form in signature (1,9), or a demonstrated equivalent explicit basis, was identified in the 32 queries and primary-source reads recorded below. This is a search outcome, not proof of absence. Review was finalized before additional follow-up research, at the coordinating auditor's instruction.

**A definite arithmetic inconsistency exists in the cited primary source.** Cederwall et al., arXiv:2509.14350v2, §4.1.4 says there are 64 new degree-twelve invariants and a cumulative total of 83. Its lower-degree inventory contributes 21, so those statements disagree. Equation (4.2) instead has degree-twelve factor exponent 62. The frozen graph manuscript uses 62 and agrees with that equation. Thus this inconsistency does not falsify the frozen 62 result. The source should be cited by equation and qualified; no correction to the published paper was independently established. [Primary text, §4.1.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.14350v2#S4.SS1.SSS4)

General interaction formulas in terms of unspecified invariant functions are not a constructive 81-invariant basis. ## Coverage limits and permissible conclusion

This was a broad, bounded search, not an exhaustive bibliographic proof. Search engines index incompletely; non-English terminology, unindexed theses, older invariant-theory classifications, private/unpublished work and equivalent constructions with unfamiliar representation notation could be missed. No exhaustive MathSciNet/zbMATH or complete citation-database traversal was performed. Some leads received only abstract-level screening, and two retrieval attempts failed. No author was contacted. Secondary/AI-generated summaries appeared as discovery leads but were not relied on for mathematical conclusions.

The strongest supported wording is: **“This audit did not identify a prior explicit 81-element functional basis in the searched primary literature; the directly relevant 2022 and 2025/2026 papers examined here do not provide such a list.”** It does not support “first,” “novel,” “the literature contains none,” or an unconditional priority claim.

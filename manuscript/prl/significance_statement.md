# Significance statement (editor-facing)

Non-linear theories of chiral form fields are built by deforming a free theory
along a stress-tensor flow. In four and six dimensions that construction reaches
everything available. In ten dimensions it does not, and that much is known.

What was not known is what it misses. We determine the space of degree-ten local
interactions of the ten-dimensional self-dual five-form exactly, show that the
stress-flow-reachable part is a subspace of codimension three, and exhibit
representatives of the three missing directions. Every product of lower-degree
invariants is reachable, so the missing directions are not assembled from
anything simpler.

The consequence is a requirement on formulation: any deformation scheme intended
to cover this dynamics at this order must add at least three independent
generators beyond the stress-tensor sector, and three are enough.

No priority claim is made. The count of 81 functionally independent invariants
and the non-universality of stress-only flows are both prior results and are
cited as such.

# Author contributions

**HUMAN ACTION REQUIRED.** Contributions are recorded factually in
`submission_candidate/CONTRIBUTION_LEDGER.csv` from commit history and run
records. That ledger deliberately does not name people: a contribution record
does not by itself determine authorship, and authorship is not ours to assign.

AI assistance is disclosed in `submission_candidate/ai_use_disclosure.md`. No AI
system is listed as an author.

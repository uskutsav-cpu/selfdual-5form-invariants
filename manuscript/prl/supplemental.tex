\section{Conventions}
Signature $(-,+,\dots,+)$; $\eps_{01\ldots9}=+1$;
$(\star F)_I = \frac{1}{(d-p)!}\eps_I{}^J F_J$. For $d=10$, $p=5$,
$\star^2=(-1)^{25}\sgn\det\eta=+1$, computed at runtime rather than assumed.
Self-duality is $\star F=+F$, projector $(1+\star)/2$ with $1/2$ the modular
inverse of $2$. Clifford normalisation $\{\Gamma^\mu,\Gamma^\nu\}=2\eta^{\mu\nu}$;
positive chirality is $\Lambda^{\rm even}W$; the antisymmetrised five-gamma
carries unit weight on the identity permutation.

Every convention is pinned as a named constant in
\texttt{sdbridge/conventions.py}; downstream code imports the constant and may not
re-derive a sign locally.

\section{Arithmetic and primes}
All ranks are exact over $\Fp$. Fitting primes $32749$, $32719$; holdout primes
$32717$, $32713$, $32707$. Modular rank bounds the characteristic-zero rank from
below unconditionally when the matrix is an integer reduction, which holds here
because the coordinate basis is integral with entries in $\{-1,0,+1\}$.
No upper bound anywhere in this work is taken from a modular computation.

\section{Sample protocol}
A registry of $\nCommonSamples$ self-dual five-forms: $\nSampleSparse$ sparse,
$\nSampleStructured$ structured, $\nSampleGeneric$ generic, $\nSampleHoldout$
holdout drawn from a disjoint seed stream. Every sample is hashed, checked for
exact self-duality, and its bridge image checked gamma-traceless before use.
Change-of-basis maps are fitted on the non-holdout samples and validated on the
holdout samples only.

\section{The bridge}
$S_{ab}=\frac{1}{5!}F_{\mu_1\ldots\mu_5}(\Gamma^{\mu_1\ldots\mu_5})_{ab}$.
Verified at each prime: kernel equals the anti-self-dual $\dimAntiSelfdual$ and
image equals the gamma-traceless $\dimGammaTraceless$, both by two-way span
containment; forward rank $\bridgeForwardRank$, not $\dimSymSpinor$; the
constructed left inverse composes with the forward map to give the self-dual
projector exactly.

The oscillator frame has real signature $(5,5)$, computed from all one hundred
anticommutators of the frame's own operators. $(5,5)$ and $(1,9)$ are
inequivalent real forms; over $\Fp$ they are congruent because both metrics have
discriminant $-1$ up to squares, and the transition matrix is constructed
explicitly rather than assumed to exist.

The spinor index placement in $\sigma^\mu_{ab}$ is undocumented upstream. It was
\emph{determined} by requiring exact equivariance: of eight candidate placements
exactly one reproduces the transformed image on every component with a single
scalar. This is a reconstruction of a convention, not a citation of one.

\section{Degree-8 span equality}
At four primes, trace rank $7$, spinor rank $7$, union rank $7$, with two-way
containment and holdout-validated change of basis. Dropping each spinor family in
turn and re-ranking: port graphs $7$, products $7$, structured $\Omega/\Theta$
$7$, \textbf{tensor words $6$}. Only the tensor words are indispensable, which
locates the direction a port-graph-only family misses.

\section{Exact Jacobian}
Analytic derivatives throughout; no finite differences. Port graphs are
differentiated by amputation, tensor words by the product rule on the trace, and
the $\Omega/\Theta$ family by exact modular interpolation cross-checked against
an independent polarisation. Euler homogeneity
$\sum_r c_r\,\partial_r I = (\deg I)\,I$ holds for every evaluated candidate.

Rank $81$; per-degree block ranks $1,2,6,12,61$; cumulative $1,3,9,21,81$. No row
normalisation is used, and over $\Fp$ none would be meaningful. Of $83$ scheduled
candidates, $82$ carry terminal status \texttt{evaluated}; one exceeded the
wall-clock budget and is recorded as \texttt{evaluation\_error} rather than
dropped. It cannot change a rank already at the analytic maximum.

\section{What is computer-assisted}
The dimensions are ranks of explicitly recorded integer matrices. The repository
ships the matrices, the pivot rows and columns, and a script that recomputes each
rank from stored data without re-running any search, so the ranks are checkable
without trusting the enumeration. The enumeration itself is the step that rests
on search; it is corroborated by an independent formula-free reverse recovery and
by the spinor computation, but it is not a symbolic proof.

\section{Software}
Three packages with no shared implementation: the tensor implementation, the
bridge, and an external third-party spinor archive that is analysed but never
redistributed. Import audits in both directions are recorded in
\texttt{docs/SOURCE\_OVERLAP\_AUDIT.md}. Implementation independence holds;
clean-room independence is not claimed.

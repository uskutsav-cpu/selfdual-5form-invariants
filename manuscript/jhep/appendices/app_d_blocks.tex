\section{Tensor building blocks}
\label{app:blocks}

\paragraph{The quadratic block.}
\begin{equation}
M_{\mu\nu} \;=\; F_{\mu\alpha\beta\gamma\delta}\,
                 F_\nu{}^{\alpha\beta\gamma\delta}.
\end{equation}
$M$ is symmetric. For self-dual $F$ its trace vanishes,
$M^\mu{}_\mu = \langle F,F\rangle = 0$, by the argument of
appendix~\ref{app:gten}; this is the same vanishing that fixes the leading degree
of the stress-tensor trace, and it means the family
$\operatorname{Tr}(M^k)$ starts contributing at $k = 2$.

\paragraph{Quartic blocks.}
From four copies of $F$ two irreducible structures are needed. We label them
$N^{(1050)}$ and $N^{(4125)}$ by the dimensions of the $\mathrm{Spin}(10)$
representations in which they transform. They are obtained by projecting the
four-fold product onto those irreducible components; the projectors are built
from $\eta$ and $\epsilon$ contractions in the fixed index ordering used
throughout.

\paragraph{Index placement.}
All blocks are stored with indices in the down position and raised only at
contraction time, so that no expression depends on an implicit metric factor.
Contractions are performed in a fixed order determined by the contraction planner,
with reduction mod $p$ after every step.

\paragraph{Why the labels matter and where they do not.}
The representation labels are descriptive. Nothing in the results depends on the
irreducible decomposition being the one the labels suggest --- the invariants are
computed by explicit contraction, and their ranks are what is certified. The
labels are retained because they make the candidate inventory of
appendix~\ref{app:bases} readable, not because any claim rests on them.

\paragraph{Products versus primitives.}
At degree ten the product subspace $\Pten$ is spanned by
$I^{(4)}_1 I^{(6)}_1$ and $\bigl(I^{(4)}_1\bigr)$-type combinations formed from
lower-degree invariants; its exact dimension is $\dimPten$. Everything not in
$\Pten$ is called primitive. The decomposition
$\Ften = \Gten \oplus \Pten$ holds with $\dim \Gten = \dimGten$ and
$\dim \Pten = \dimPten$, summing to $\dimAtenQ$, and the graph generators are
therefore a complement to the products.

\section{The generic-rank certificate}
\label{app:rank81}

\paragraph{Coordinate basis.}
The $\dimSelfDualModule$ coordinates are the independent components of a
self-dual five-form in a fixed ordering of index five-tuples. All derivatives are
taken with respect to these, so the Jacobian is
$\exactJacRows \times \exactJacCols$.

\paragraph{Candidate ordering.}
The $\exactJacScheduled$ candidates are ordered by a fixed rule and the ordering
is hashed. Every cell records the hash, and cells whose hashes differ are not
comparable. All $\matrixCells$ cells aggregated here share one ordering hash.

\paragraph{Schedule completeness.}
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lc}
\hline
scheduled & $\exactJacScheduled$ \\
evaluated & $\exactJacEvaluated$ \\
errors & $\exactJacErrors$ \\
interrupted & $0$ \\
silently skipped & $0$ \\
identically-zero rows & $\exactJacZeroRows$ \\
Euler homogeneity & $\exactJacEulerPass$ \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
The distinction between ``evaluated'' and ``scheduled'' is tracked explicitly
because an earlier stage of this project reported a rank from an incomplete
schedule, with the missing candidates invisible in the output.

\paragraph{Prime and sample design.}
$\matrixPrimes$ primes $\times$ $\matrixSeeds$ seeds $= \matrixCells$ cells. The
primes are near $2^{15}$, chosen so that products of two residues fit in machine
integers without overflow; the seeds control the sample point.

\paragraph{Pivots.}
Every cell returns the same $\matrixStableRows$ pivot rows and
$\matrixStableCols$ pivot columns, with $\matrixUnstableRows$ unstable. The pivot
row identities are recorded as candidate identifiers, not as indices, so the
comparison is meaningful across runs.

\paragraph{The minor.}
An explicit $\minorSize \times \minorSize$ submatrix is fixed and its determinant
computed at prime $\minorPrimes$. The determinant is non-zero, and it is computed
by two independent routines --- fraction-free Bareiss elimination and a separate
exact routine --- which agree.

\paragraph{From the minor to characteristic zero.}
The chosen submatrix has integer entries, being an integer reduction of an
analytic derivative evaluated at an integer point. Its determinant is therefore an
integer $D$, and the computation shows $D \not\equiv 0 \bmod p$. Hence
$D \neq 0$ over $\mathbb{Z}$, so the corresponding minor over $\Q$ is non-zero and
\begin{equation}
\text{generic functional rank} \;\ge\; \charZeroLowerBound .
\end{equation}
No prime is excluded and no genericity assumption on the sample point is needed:
a specific point exhibiting rank $\charZeroLowerBound$ bounds the generic rank
from below.

\paragraph{The upper bound.}
The matching upper bound $\genericFunctionalDim$ is analytic, following from the
generic orbit dimension of the Lorentz action on
$\Lambda^5_+$~\cite{Hutomo:2025chiral}. We cite it and do not reprove it. The
combination of the two gives the exact value, and we are explicit about which
half is ours.

\paragraph{Scope.}
The certified statement is that \emph{the selected family has generic functional
rank $\genericFunctionalDim$}. It is not the statement that all
$\exactJacScheduled$ candidates are algebraically independent: functional
independence at a generic point and algebraic independence are different
properties, and only the former is established here.

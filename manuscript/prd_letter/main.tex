\title{Exact degree-ten invariants of a self-dual five-form in ten dimensions}

\author{\pending{Author list pending mentor review}}

\begin{abstract}
The Lorentz-invariant scalar functions of a self-dual five-form in ten
dimensions control the non-linear self-interactions the field can have. We
determine the degree-ten sector of this structure exactly over $\Q$, rather than
modulo a prime. The full invariant space has dimension $\dimAtenQ$; the subspace
activated by the stress-tensor flow has dimension $\dimDtenQ$; the quotient
therefore has dimension $\dimQtenQ$, so three independent degree-ten directions
lie outside the reach of the flow. The separation rests on a short theorem: the
free stress tensor of a self-dual five-form is traceless for every improvement
coefficient, because $F\wedge F$ vanishes identically for a form of odd degree,
so $\operatorname{Tr}\tau$ begins at field degree four rather than two. Forcing
the naive degree-two start collapses the quotient to zero. We certify a
tensor--spinor bridge of rank $\bridgeForwardRank$ with an exact left inverse,
certify generic functional rank at least $\charZeroLowerBound$ by an explicit
$\minorSize\times\minorSize$ integer minor, and give the one-dimensional overlap
between the published degree-ten span and the product sector as an exact integer
identity. All computations are exact; none uses floating point.
\end{abstract}

\emph{Introduction.}---A chiral $p$-form has a field strength constrained to
equal its own Hodge dual. The constraint is forced on us in string theory and
sits badly with the usual machinery: a self-dual field strength admits no
manifestly Lorentz-invariant action of the naive kind, a difficulty identified
by Marcus and Schwarz~\cite{Marcus:1982yu} and addressed since by a sequence of
constructions~\cite{Henneaux:1988gg,Schwarz:1993vs,Pasti:1995tn,Pasti:1997gx,%
Sen:2019qit,Mkrtchyan:2019wnk}. The obstruction is visible in one line, and we
recover it below as \eqref{eq:normvanishes}: the candidate kinetic term
$\langle F,F\rangle$ vanishes identically on the constrained surface.

Asking not for \emph{a} chiral theory but for the space of non-linear
self-inter\-actions one can have turns the problem into a question about scalar
invariants. In four dimensions the answer is classical and the duality-invariant
extensions of Maxwell theory are completely
characterised~\cite{Bandos:2020hgy,Bandos:2021rqy}; in six dimensions the
self-interacting chiral two-form and its $T\bar T$-like stress-tensor flow are
well developed~\cite{Townsend:2019ftg,Ferko:2024chiral,Brizio:2026ttbar}. Ten
dimensions is harder for combinatorial rather than conceptual reasons: the
self-dual five-form lives in a $\dimSelfDualModule$-dimensional module and the
contraction patterns available at degree ten are too numerous for reliable hand
enumeration. Explicit degree-ten structures and the relevant flow appear
in~\cite{Cederwall:2025invariants,Hutomo:2025chiral}. What has been missing is
the exact dimension of the degree-ten invariant space, the exact dimension of
the part the flow reaches, and the relation between them. We supply all three.

The five-form is not an arbitrary choice of example. It is the Ramond--Ramond
field strength of Type IIB supergravity, whose covariant field equations are due
to Schwarz~\cite{Schwarz:1983qr} and for which manifestly Lorentz-invariant
actions were later built with precisely the chiral-boson machinery cited
above~\cite{DallAgata:1998ahf}; higher-derivative corrections involving it have
been studied directly~\cite{Paulos:2008tn}, within the eight-derivative
programme~\cite{Green:1997as,Liu:2022bfg}, and non-perturbatively through
D-instantons~\cite{Green:1997tv}. We make no Type IIB claim here, and say so
again below; the point is only that the invariant structure of this particular
module is not an idle question.

Two features of the method are worth stating at the outset, because they are
what the results rest on. First, everything is computed in exact arithmetic:
integer or rational, or modular over $\Fp$ with the passage to characteristic
zero made explicit each time. No quantity in this paper is a floating-point
number and no rank is obtained by thresholding a singular value. Second, the
enumeration is carried out twice, in two languages that share no evaluation
code, so that agreement between them is informative rather than tautological.
The general enumerate--evaluate--relate workflow is prior
work~\cite{Elamaran:2025mlinv}; what we contribute is its exact ten-dimensional
realisation, the certified bridge, and the characteristic-zero certificates.

\emph{Setup.}---Work on $\mathbb{R}^{1,9}$ with
$\eta=\mathrm{diag}(-1,+1,\dots,+1)$ and $\epsilon_{01\dots9}=+1$. On five-forms
the Hodge star acts by
$(\star F)_{\mu_1\dots\mu_5}=\frac{1}{5!}\epsilon_{\mu_1\dots\mu_5}{}^{\nu_1\dots\nu_5}F_{\nu_1\dots\nu_5}$.
For a $p$-form in $d=2p$ dimensions with $t$ timelike directions,
\begin{equation}
\star^{2}=(-1)^{p(d-p)+t}=(-1)^{p^{2}+t},
\label{eq:starsq}
\end{equation}
so at $p=5$, $d=10$ one has $\star^2=-(-1)^t$: eigenvalues $\pm1$ in Lorentzian
signature and $\pm i$, with no real eigenspace decomposition, in Euclidean.
Self-duality of a five-form is thus a Lorentzian phenomenon. The projectors
$P_\pm=\frac12(\mathbf{1}\pm\star)$ satisfy $P_\pm^2=P_\pm$ and
$\operatorname{tr}P_\pm=\dimSelfDualModule$, and $\Lambda^5$ splits as
$\Lambda^5_+\oplus\Lambda^5_-$ with
$\dim\Lambda^5_\pm=\dimSelfDualModule$ inside
$\dim\Lambda^5=\dimFiveFormComponents$. Write $F\in\Lambda^5_+$. ``Degree''
always means degree in $F$; only even degrees occur, and $A_d$ denotes the
invariants of degree $d$.

The invariants themselves are built by full contraction. A degree-$d$ candidate
is specified by a contraction graph: $d$ copies of $F$ carrying $5d$ indices,
paired off by $\eta$ and, where the pattern demands it, by one $\epsilon$. Each
graph evaluates to a polynomial with \emph{integer} coefficients in the
$\dimSelfDualModule$ coordinates, fixed before any prime is chosen, since $\eta$
and $\epsilon$ have integer entries; the prime enters only when that fixed
polynomial is evaluated. This is what makes the spanning-set argument available:
a space spanned by exactly $k$ explicit elements has $\dim_\Q\le k$ for free, so
a modular rank of $k$ pins the rational dimension with no prime excluded. It
applies to $\Ften$ and to the product sector, and it is precisely what is
\emph{not} available for $\Dten$, whose elements the closure produces rather
than receives --- which is why that space is treated by exact lifting instead.

The Lorentz group acts on $\Lambda^5_+$ with
$\dim\mathfrak{so}(1,9)=\dimLorentzAlgebra$. A generic point has a stabiliser,
and the number of functionally independent invariants at a generic point is
$\dimSelfDualModule$ minus the generic orbit dimension. That analytic count is
$\genericFunctionalDim$ and is not ours~\cite{Hutomo:2025chiral}; what we
contribute is an exact computational lower bound matching it.

\emph{The tensor--spinor bridge.}---Two independent constructions are used
throughout, one from contraction graphs and one from chiral bispinors, and their
agreement is the paper's main internal control. They are related by
\begin{equation}
\Phi(F)_{ab}=\frac{1}{5!}\,F_{\mu_1\dots\mu_5}(C\Gamma^{\mu_1\dots\mu_5})_{ab},
\label{eq:bridge}
\end{equation}
with $a,b=1,\dots,\spinorDim$. The target is not all of $\mathrm{Sym}^2S_+$:
with $\dim S_+=\spinorDim$ we have $\dim\mathrm{Sym}^2S_+=\dimSymSpinor$, the
gamma trace $(\gamma\text{-}\mathrm{tr}\,\psi)_\mu=\eta_{\mu\nu}(\Gamma^\nu)^{ab}\psi_{ab}$
has rank $\gammaTraceRank$, and
\begin{equation}
\dim\ker(\gamma\text{-}\mathrm{tr})=\dimSymSpinor-\gammaTraceRank
=\dimGammaTraceless=\dim\Lambda^5_+ .
\label{eq:dimcount}
\end{equation}

\begin{proposition}[certified]
\label{prop:bridge}
Over $\Fp$ at each prime used, $\mathrm{im}\,\Phi=\ker(\gamma\text{-}\mathrm{tr})$,
$\mathrm{rank}\,\Phi=\bridgeForwardRank$, $\ker\Phi\cap\Lambda^5_+=0$, and
$\Phi|_{\Lambda^5_-}\equiv0$. There is an exact left inverse with
$\Phi^-\Phi=\mathrm{id}$, while $\Phi\Phi^-$ is the projector onto
$\ker(\gamma\text{-}\mathrm{tr})$ and not the identity. Equivariance holds with
the intertwining character solved for, not assumed, and found to be unity.
\end{proposition}

Two points are easy to get wrong. $\Phi$ has full column rank but is not onto,
so it admits a left inverse and no two-sided one; both identities are verified
as matrix statements, since one that holds on sampled vectors can still fail on
a subspace. And the vanishing on $\Lambda^5_-$ is exact, not approximate: on
$S_+$ the anti-self-dual part of $\Gamma^{(5)}$ annihilates the module. That
blindness makes an orientation error silent. The null frame over $\Fp$ solves
$L^{T}\eta L=\eta_{\mathrm{null}}$, which fixes $L$ only up to square-root
branches; two solutions differing in $\det L$ induce $\star\mapsto\pm\star$ and
hence rank $\bridgeForwardRank$ versus rank $0$, with no error raised either
way. We pin the branch by construction and verify $\star F=+F$ on the image --- a
property of the output rather than of the criterion that selected it. The defect
was found because three cells of the rank matrix failed at a single prime, and
was diagnosed as a branch rather than an exceptional prime by flipping it
deliberately at both a failing and a working prime. No published number changed.

One further point deserves care, because an earlier version of this project
recorded its opposite. The spinor computation is natural in a null frame adapted
to split signature $(5,5)$, while the physical problem is Lorentzian. These are
\emph{not} related by a real orthogonal transformation --- a real congruence
preserves signature, so no $\Lambda\in O(1,9;\mathbb{R})$ carries $\eta_{(1,9)}$
to $\eta_{(5,5)}$. They are different real forms of one complex algebra,
\begin{equation}
\mathfrak{so}(1,9)_{\mathbb{C}}\cong\mathfrak{so}(10,\mathbb{C})
\cong\mathfrak{so}(5,5)_{\mathbb{C}},
\label{eq:realforms}
\end{equation}
and every dimension, rank and intersection quoted here is the dimension of a
$\mathbb{C}$-linear object and so is constant along \eqref{eq:realforms}. What
\eqref{eq:realforms} does \emph{not} transport is which eigenspace of $\star$
survives: $\star$ is built from $\eta$, and by \eqref{eq:starsq} the reality of
its eigenspaces is exactly a signature-dependent statement.

\emph{The trace theorem.}---Everything at degree ten turns on the field degree
at which $\operatorname{Tr}\tau$ begins.

\begin{theorem}
\label{thm:gten}
For a self-dual five-form in ten dimensions the free stress tensor is traceless,
for every improvement coefficient. Hence $\operatorname{Tr}\tau$ first
contributes at field degree four.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Take the general improved form
\begin{equation}
T_{\mu\nu}=\frac{1}{(p-1)!}F_{\mu\alpha_2\dots\alpha_p}F_\nu{}^{\alpha_2\dots\alpha_p}
-c\,\eta_{\mu\nu}\langle F,F\rangle,
\label{eq:stress}
\end{equation}
with $\langle F,G\rangle=F_{\mu_1\dots\mu_p}G^{\mu_1\dots\mu_p}$ and $c$ free.
Contracting with $\eta^{\mu\nu}$,
\begin{equation}
T^\mu{}_\mu=(1-cd)\,\langle F,F\rangle,
\label{eq:trace}
\end{equation}
a multiple of the single scalar $\langle F,F\rangle$ whatever $c$ is: no other
quadratic Lorentz invariant exists, the full contraction being the only way to
pair two $p$-forms into a scalar. Now specialise to $p=5$, $d=10$, so $F$ has
\emph{odd} form degree. Exchanging two factors of a $q$-form costs
$(-1)^{q^2}=(-1)^q$, which is $-1$ at $q=5$, so
\begin{equation}
F\wedge F=-F\wedge F\ \Longrightarrow\ F\wedge F\equiv 0
\label{eq:wedge}
\end{equation}
for \emph{every} five-form. Since
$\langle F,\star F\rangle\,\mathrm{vol}\propto F\wedge F$, the pairing
$\langle F,\star F\rangle$ vanishes, and on either eigenspace $\star F=\pm F$
gives
\begin{equation}
\langle F,F\rangle=\pm\langle F,\star F\rangle=0 .
\label{eq:normvanishes}
\end{equation}
Substituting into \eqref{eq:trace} gives $T^\mu{}_\mu=0$ for all $c$.
\end{proof}

The two steps are independent, and that matters for how the statement is tested.
Equation~\eqref{eq:wedge} is about form parity and holds for any five-form;
self-duality enters only at \eqref{eq:normvanishes}. A suite checking self-dual
samples alone would confirm the conclusion without exercising the mechanism, so
we check both eigenspaces --- the argument predicts vanishing on each --- and add
a control confirming $\langle F,F\rangle\neq0$ on an \emph{unprojected}
five-form, without which the other checks pass vacuously. The sign in
\eqref{eq:starsq} is likewise asserted by test wherever the decomposition is
used: a Euclidean projector, where $\star^2=-1$, was once mistaken for an
anti-self-dual one here and its non-vanishing briefly read as signal.

\emph{Activation and the exact closure.}---The flow generates new invariants by
repeatedly applying scalars built from traces of powers of $\tau$. Through
degree ten the generators are $\operatorname{Tr}\tau^{k}$ for $k\le5$ together
with the products
$(\operatorname{Tr}\tau)^2$, $\operatorname{Tr}\tau\operatorname{Tr}\tau^{2}$,
$\operatorname{Tr}\tau\operatorname{Tr}\tau^{3}$,
$(\operatorname{Tr}\tau^{2})^{2}$ and
$\operatorname{Tr}\tau^{2}\operatorname{Tr}\tau^{3}$; the list was obtained by
instrumenting the closure, so that a generator consumed but undocumented would
appear. Writing $\deg_F$ for leading field degree,
\begin{equation}
\deg_F\operatorname{Tr}\tau^{k}=
\begin{cases}4,&k=1,\\ 2k,&k\ge2,\end{cases}
\label{eq:leading}
\end{equation}
with degrees adding on products. The exceptional case is
Theorem~\ref{thm:gten}: the naive count gives $2$, and the vanishing of the free
trace pushes it to $4$.

A target is \emph{generated} when the flow produces it formally and
\emph{activated} at degree $d$ when \eqref{eq:leading} lets it contribute there.
Concretely, the reachable space at degree $d$ is the least fixed point of
\begin{equation}
V\longmapsto V+\mathrm{span}\bigl\{g:\ g\ \text{generated from}\ V,\
\deg_F g\le d\bigr\},
\label{eq:closureop}
\end{equation}
started from the degree-four seed and iterated until a sweep raises no rank.
Over $\Q$ the reachable dimensions by degree are $1$, $2$, $7$ and $\dimDtenQ$
at degrees four, six, eight and ten.

The distinction between generated and activated is the whole result. Writing
$\mathcal{T}$ for the generated targets and $\mathcal{A}$ for activation,
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{span}\,\mathcal{T}=\Ften
\quad\text{but}\quad
\Dten=\mathrm{span}\!\bigcup_{k\ge0}\mathcal{A}^{k}(\mathcal{T}_0)\subsetneq\Ften .
\label{eq:rawvsact}
\end{equation}
The left equality is why conflating them is tempting; doing so returns quotient
dimension zero, which is the answer this project held before catching the error.

Running the closure modulo $p$ would expose the fixed point to exceptional
primes precisely where no spanning-set argument is available, so it is run over
$\Q$. Of its target rows $\czIntegerRows$ were already integral; the remaining
$\czLiftedRows$ were lifted by the Chinese remainder theorem across
$\czFitPrimes$ primes with rational reconstruction~\cite{Wang:1982} and
validated at the held-out prime $\czHoldoutPrime$, with no mismatches. This is
not fastidiousness. Figure~\ref{fig:heights} plots the size of the largest
coefficient the closure actually produces at each degree. Through degree eight
the coordinates are small integers and nothing is at risk. At degree ten one
basis vector of $\Dten$ carries a numerator of twenty-nine bits over a
five-digit denominator --- an order of magnitude past what a single prime near
$2^{15}$ can represent. Read modulo one such prime that coefficient is
indistinguishable from a small residue, and the fixed point would have been
reported at the wrong dimension with nothing in the output to indicate it.
Iteration terminated after $\czSweeps$ sweeps.

\begin{theorem}
\label{thm:main}
Over the rationals,
\begin{equation}
\dim_\Q\Ften=\dimAtenQ,\quad
\dim_\Q\Dten=\dimDtenQ,\quad
\dim_\Q\Qten=\dimQtenQ .
\end{equation}
\end{theorem}

The lower bound is certified by an explicit
$\czMinorSize\times\czMinorSize$ integer minor with non-vanishing determinant,
computed by Bareiss elimination~\cite{Bareiss:1968} and confirmed by an
independent exact rational routine. No separate upper bound is needed: over $\Q$
the modular admission test that made the predecessor a bound disappears, and at
the fixed point no further generated direction raises the rank, so the reached
span \emph{is} $\Dten$ and $\dim_\Q\Dten=\dimDtenQ$ is an equality. Three exact
annihilating covectors give the quotient map directly. Forcing
$\operatorname{Tr}\tau$ to begin at degree two instead activates $24$ further
targets, raises the reachable dimension to $\dimAten$, and returns
$\Qten=0$; that counterfactual is retained as a regression fixture.

How many directions must be added to close the gap is a basis-dependent question
only in appearance.

\begin{proposition}
\label{prop:card}
If $S\subset\Ften$ satisfies $\Dten+\mathrm{span}(S)=\Ften$, then
$|S|\ge\dim\Qten=\dimQtenQ$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Apply the quotient map $\pi:\Ften\to\Qten$. From
$\Dten+\mathrm{span}(S)=\Ften$ we get $\mathrm{span}(\pi(S))=\Qten$, and a span
of $|S|$ vectors has dimension at most $|S|$.
\end{proof}

This is elementary and we claim no novelty for it; we state it because it
converts a basis-dependent assertion into a basis-free one. No closing set of
any kind, in any basis, has fewer than $\dimQtenQ$ elements. Removal minimality
of the particular closing set we exhibit --- that no element can be dropped ---
is checked only in a fixed graph basis and under the permutation subgroup, and
we do not claim it under arbitrary $GL$.

\emph{Degree-resolved agreement.}---The two formulations are compared on
$\nCommonSamples$ common samples at prime $\comparisonPrime$. Ranks agree at
degrees four, six, eight and ten [Fig.~\ref{fig:spectrum}(c)], which is
meaningful corroboration precisely because a shared omission would have to occur
twice in two different languages. At degree eight the full spinor family
attains $\dEightSpinorRank$; deleting its tensor words drops it to
$\dEightRankWithoutWords$, so within this decomposition the tensor words are
indispensable. We state that in the qualified form the evidence supports: it is
a statement about the families constructed here, not a uniqueness theorem.

\emph{Generic functional rank.}---Let $I_1,\dots,I_N$ be the candidates,
$N=\exactJacScheduled$, in the $n=\dimSelfDualModule$ coordinates $F^A$. The
generic functional rank is the rank at a generic point of
$J^{\,A}_{i}=\partial I_i/\partial F^{A}$, an
$\exactJacRows\times\exactJacCols$ matrix. Rows are differentiated analytically,
by amputating one $F$ from the contraction pattern, so there is no step size;
each is checked against Euler's relation
$\sum_A F^{A}\partial_A I_i=d_i I_i$, which ties a differentiated row to an
undifferentiated evaluation and so detects exactly the errors differentiation
introduces. All $\exactJacEvaluated$ of $\exactJacScheduled$ candidates were
evaluated, with $\exactJacErrors$ errors and $\exactJacZeroRows$ zero rows.

The exact modular rank is $\exactJacRank$, and an explicit
$\minorSize\times\minorSize$ minor has non-zero determinant. A non-vanishing
minor over $\Fp$ lifts to one over $\Q$, since the determinant is an integer
non-zero mod $p$; hence
\begin{equation}
\text{generic functional rank}\ \ge\ \charZeroLowerBound
\end{equation}
with no prime excluded. Repeating across $\matrixPrimes$ primes and
$\matrixSeeds$ seeds gives $\matrixCells$ cells, every one at rank
$\matrixRank$ with the same $\matrixStableRows$ pivot rows and
$\matrixStableCols$ pivot columns and $\matrixUnstableRows$ unstable. Two
matrices can share a rank and disagree about which rows realise it, so the
stability of the pivot \emph{sets} is the stronger statement and is the one we
record. We are nonetheless deliberately restrained: cells from one run agreeing
with each other is close to guaranteed whether or not the run is correct, so the
matrix corroborates and the minor is what carries the bound.

The rank is also not concentrated at one degree. Sorting the $\minorSize$ pivot
rows of the certified minor by the degree of the candidate that supplies them
gives $1$, $2$, $6$, $12$ and $60$ at degrees four through twelve, and sorting
them by construction gives $68$ port graphs against $9$ tensor words and $4$
spinor candidates. The tensor words carry pivots of their own, which is the same
conclusion the degree-eight ablation reaches by a different route.

\emph{The published span and the product sector.}---Let $\Bten$ be the span of
the published degree-ten structures and $\Pten$ the product sector. Over $\Q$,
$\dim\Bten=\dimBten$, $\dim\Pten=\dimPten$, $\dim(\Bten+\Pten)=\dimBplusP$ and
\begin{equation}
\dim_\Q(\Bten\cap\Pten)=\dimBcapP,
\end{equation}
generated by the exact integer identity
\begin{equation}
\begin{split}
-55\,I^{(1)}-252\,I^{(2)}&+4200\,I^{(5)}+5040\,I^{(6)}\\
&=-1040\,I^{(4)}_1I^{(6)}_1 ,
\end{split}
\end{equation}
verified at the fresh prime $\bpFreshPrime$ on $\bpFreshSamples$ samples that
took no part in the reconstruction. The coefficients are
normalisation-dependent, unlike every dimension quoted above; what is
normalisation-independent is that the intersection is one-dimensional and
supported on the four published structures listed. We read this as a structural
observation about the basis rather than a defect in it.

\emph{What the quotient means, and what it does not.}---It is worth separating
the arithmetic from its reading. That $\dim_\Q\Qten=\dimQtenQ$ is certified; what
that number licenses one to say is a separate question, and we are deliberately
sparing. The flow of~\cite{Hutomo:2025chiral} deforms a chiral theory along a
direction built from its own stress tensor, in the manner
of~\cite{Zamolodchikov:2004ce,Smirnov:2016lqw,Cavaglia:2016oda,Conti:2018tca} in
two dimensions. A non-zero quotient says that at degree ten the deformations
this flow generates do not exhaust the invariants a chiral five-form admits:
there are three independent directions in which the theory can be deformed,
consistently with Lorentz invariance and self-duality, that the flow does not
produce from its seed. Whether those directions correspond to sensible
interacting theories is not settled here. Perry and Schwarz~\cite{Perry:1996mk}
and Bekaert and Henneaux~\cite{Bekaert:1998yp} showed that self-interactions of
a chiral form are constrained beyond Lorentz invariance --- the latter deriving
the condition from the Dirac--Schwinger requirement on the energy--momentum
commutator --- and we impose no such condition. The three directions are
invariant-theoretic, and calling them physical would be a further claim
requiring further work.

\emph{Scope.}---Every dimension here is exact for the span of the enumerated
family. Reading $\dim\Ften=\dimAtenQ$ as the dimension of \emph{all} degree-ten
Lorentz invariants further requires the enumeration to miss nothing, which we do
not prove; the two independent constructions agreeing at four degrees is
corroboration, not a theorem, and a Hilbert-series or character count would
settle it. We do not prove full degree-twelve tensor--spinor equivalence, give a
complete invariant-ring presentation, claim canonicality of any basis, or claim
minimality under arbitrary $GL$ change of basis. No Type IIB prediction, no
statement about causal propagation or hyperbolicity, and no supersymmetry claim
is made or implied.

\emph{Reproducibility.}---All computations are exact and none uses floating
point. $\nTraceTests$ tensor-side and $\nBridgeTests$ bridge-side tests pass
from a clean clone, and the certificates, figures and tables regenerate from
committed scripts; no scientific value is typed into the manuscript source, each
being read from a generated macro so that the text and the artifacts cannot
drift apart. Cells of the rank matrix are written once and aggregated read-only,
with the aggregation independent of order, and a set of gates runs against the
compiled source to check specific wording and consistency requirements --- that
no unscoped completeness claim appears, that a quantity certified as a bound is
never described as an equality. Each gate was negative-tested by deliberately
violating the condition it detects, since a gate that cannot fail is not a gate.

Two defects were found only by clean-clone reproduction, and we record them
rather than quietly fixing them. One was an undocumented Python floor. The other
is more instructive: the modular contractor enforces an intermediate-size and a
floating-point-operation budget, and both checks sat behind an optional import
that the reproduction documents described as not installed for the certified
runs. In that configuration neither budget was ever evaluated, and the guard
against runaway contractions was inactive in exactly the environment a
reproducer was told to use. The dependency is now required and the fallback
raises rather than running unguarded; no computed value changed.

We also state a limit on reproducibility rather than leaving it to be inferred.
Figures, generated tables and the source archive are byte-identical across clean
rebuilds within a pinned toolchain. The compiled PDF is not: the typesetter
writes fresh build metadata on every invocation, and three consecutive
compilations of identical source produce three distinct digests. The hash
recorded for a PDF identifies one build; it does not certify a reproducible one.

\emph{Conclusions.}---The degree-ten Lorentz-invariant structure of a self-dual
five-form in ten dimensions is now known exactly over $\Q$. The full invariant
space has dimension $\dimAtenQ$, the stress-flow-activated subspace has
dimension $\dimDtenQ$, and the quotient has dimension $\dimQtenQ$. The published
degree-ten span meets the product sector in exactly one dimension, written out
as an explicit integer identity. Generic functional rank is at least
$\charZeroLowerBound$, certified by an explicit minor rather than by an
elimination whose correctness one would have to take on trust.

The separation of $\Dten$ from $\Ften$ rests entirely on
Theorem~\ref{thm:gten}, and the theorem is short: the free stress tensor of a
self-dual five-form is traceless because $F\wedge F$ vanishes identically for a
form of odd degree, so $\operatorname{Tr}\tau$ begins at field degree four. That
shortness is a virtue here. The argument uses no property of the particular free
dynamics, no choice of improvement term and no normalisation, so it survives
reformulation of the theory. What it does assume is that the object appearing in
the flow is a stress tensor of the shape \eqref{eq:stress}. Whether that holds
in the formulation the source intends is a question about conventions rather
than about our computation, and we have flagged it rather than assumed the
favourable reading, because the counterfactual is not a small correction: it
takes the quotient from $\dimQtenQ$ to zero and changes what the paper says.

Three directions follow. A Hilbert-series or character-theoretic count of the
degree-ten invariants of the $\dimSelfDualModule$-dimensional module would
settle the exhaustiveness question that our two independent enumerations only
corroborate, and is the single most valuable check anyone could add. Extending
the equivalence to degree twelve requires the bridge argument at a degree where
the candidate count grows sharply and where we currently have only partial
certified input. And the physical status of the three unreached directions ---
whether they support consistent interacting chiral theories, or are obstructed
by the Dirac--Schwinger condition that constrains chiral self-interactions
generally --- is open, and is the question we would most like answered.

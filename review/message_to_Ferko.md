# Draft for your review

Dear Dr. Ferko,

Attached is a draft on Lorentz invariants of a self-dual five-form in ten
dimensions. It is circulated for your review only. **Your name does not appear
in the author metadata**, and it will not unless you say in writing that you
want it there.

## Why you are being asked

Three reasons, in decreasing order of how much they need you.

1. **Your paper is the method's prior art.** The enumerate-evaluate-relate
   workflow for tensor invariants is Elamaran, Ferko and Scarlett, PRD 114
   (2026) 026016. The draft says so in the introduction and claims only the
   exact and certified realisation for this particular representation. If that
   framing understates or overstates what you did, please correct it.

2. **Two conventions need a physicist's eye.** The bridge's Hodge and chirality
   channel, and the definition of the degree-ten reachable space $D_{10}$.
   Both are internally consistent and neither has been checked by anyone who
   works in this area.

3. **The credit questions are yours to answer, not ours.** Whether you should
   be an author, an acknowledged mentor, or neither, is question 10 on the
   decision form and it is deliberately left blank.
